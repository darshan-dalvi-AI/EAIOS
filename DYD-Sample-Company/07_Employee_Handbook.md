# Employee Handbook — DYD Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

**Version:** 6.1 · **Effective:** 1 April 2026 · **Owner:** People & Culture (Divya Reddy)

## 1. Leave

| Type | Entitlement | Carry forward | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Annual leave | 24 days per year, credited monthly at 2/month | Up to 10 days | Encashment only at exit |
| Casual leave | 7 days | None | Maximum 2 consecutive days |
| Sick leave | 12 days | None | Certificate required beyond 2 consecutive days |
| Maternity | 26 weeks | — | Per Maternity Benefit Act |
| Paternity | 15 days | — | Within 6 months of birth |
| Bereavement | 5 days | — | Immediate family |
| Sabbatical | Up to 3 months unpaid | — | After 4 years' service, subject to approval |

Annual leave needs 7 days' notice for 1–2 days, and 21 days' notice for anything
longer. Leave during a client go-live window requires Delivery Manager approval —
declined only with a written reason.

## 2. Working arrangements

- Hybrid: minimum **8 days per month** in office for Pune and Bengaluru staff.
- Fully remote roles exist and are named as such in the offer letter.
- Core collaboration hours 11:00–17:00 IST. Outside those, asynchronous is the default.
- On-call is compensated: ₹1,500 per weekday night, ₹3,000 per weekend day, plus
  time off in lieu for any incident worked past two hours outside working hours.
- No engineer is on call for more than one week in four.

## 3. Compensation and appraisal

- Annual cycle, effective 1 July, based on the April–March performance year.
- Mid-year check-in in November — no rating, no impact on pay, and deliberately so.
- Bands L1–L4 (individual contributor) and M1–M3 (management). Both tracks reach
  the same compensation ceiling; the M track is not a promotion from L.
- Promotion requires demonstrated performance at the next band for two consecutive
  quarters, evidenced by work, not by tenure.
- Referral bonus ₹75,000, paid after the referred employee completes 6 months.
- Certification reimbursement up to ₹60,000 per year on pass; failures are not
  charged back.

## 4. Notice period

| Band | Notice |
|---|---|
| L1–L2 | 45 days |
| L3–L4 | 60 days |
| M1–M3 | 90 days |

Buyout is permitted at the discretion of the reporting manager and People &
Culture. Garden leave may be applied where the departure is to a named competitor.

## 5. Utilisation and bench

Billable staff carry a target utilisation of **80%**. Utilisation is measured
against available hours, excluding approved leave and training days — a common
misreading is to count leave as non-billable, which understates the number.

Bench time beyond 30 days triggers a structured plan: reskilling, an internal
project, or redeployment. Bench is a management failure to solve, not an employee
failure to explain. As at July 2026, **11 employees have exceeded 30 bench days**,
representing 562 bench days and roughly ₹76 lakh of idle cost — the single largest
controllable drain on margin this year.

## 6. Conduct

- Zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination or retaliation.
- Client confidentiality survives employment, indefinitely.
- Outside work must be declared and must not compete with DYD.
- Gifts above ₹5,000 from a client or vendor must be declared.
- Grievances go to People & Culture, or to the Ethics Committee where the
  grievance concerns People & Culture. Both routes protect the reporter.
