# Employee Handbook — DYD Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

**Version 2026.1** · Owner: Nisha Gupta (HR Manager) · Approved by Meera Joshi (CEO)

This handbook is the single place to look before asking. If something here contradicts your
offer letter, the offer letter wins for terms specific to you.

---

## 1. Leave

### Annual leave
Every full-time employee receives **24 days of paid annual leave per year**, credited monthly
at 2 days per completed month. Up to **10 unused days carry forward** into the next calendar
year; anything above 10 lapses on 31 December.

Leave is applied for at least **five working days in advance** for one to three days, and
**fifteen working days in advance** for anything longer. Approval rests with your reporting
manager, and is not unreasonably withheld.

### Sick leave
**12 days of paid sick leave per year**, not carried forward. A medical certificate is required
for any absence of three consecutive days or more. Sick leave is never deducted from annual
leave.

### Parental leave
- **Maternity leave: 26 weeks paid**, in line with the Maternity Benefit Act.
- **Paternity leave: 15 working days paid**, taken within six months of birth or adoption.
- Adoption leave matches maternity provisions for the primary caregiver.

### Bereavement and emergency leave
Five days paid bereavement leave for an immediate family member. Emergency leave is at the
manager's discretion and is not deducted where circumstances are genuinely exceptional.

## 2. Working arrangements

DYD operates a **hybrid model: three days in office, two days remote**, per week. Core hours
are 11:00 to 17:00 IST — meetings are scheduled inside that window so remote and office staff
overlap reliably.

Client-site deployment may require full-time presence for defined periods; this is agreed with
the employee in advance and attracts a site allowance of ₹1,200 per day.

## 3. Expenses

| Category | Limit | Approval |
|---|---|---|
| Domestic travel, per trip | 25,000 | Reporting manager |
| International travel, per trip | 150,000 | Department head |
| Client entertainment, per event | 15,000 | Department head |
| Home office equipment, one-time | 40,000 | HR Manager |
| Certification and training, per year | 60,000 | Reporting manager |
| Mobile and internet, per month | 2,500 | Automatic |

Claims are submitted within **30 days** of expenditure with receipts. Claims older than 60 days
are not reimbursed except with written CEO approval. Reimbursement is paid with the following
month's salary.

## 4. Performance and progression

Formal reviews run twice yearly, in April and October. Ratings are: Exceptional, Strong,
Meeting Expectations, Developing, Below Expectations. Promotion decisions are made only at the
April cycle. Salary revisions take effect from 1 July.

An employee rated Below Expectations enters a documented 90-day improvement plan with weekly
check-ins. The plan states specific, measurable outcomes; it is not a formality preceding exit.

## 5. Notice and exit

| Level | Notice period |
|---|---|
| Engineer and Analyst | 60 days |
| Senior Engineer and Manager | 90 days |
| Head of Department and above | 120 days |

Notice may be reduced by mutual written agreement. Buy-out of notice is at the company's
discretion and is charged at actual salary for the un-served period.

We run an exit conversation for everyone, and what is said in it is reported only as themes,
never attributed.

## 6. Information security — mandatory

- Client data is **never** copied to personal devices, personal cloud storage or personal email.
- Production credentials are held only in the company password manager, never in code,
  configuration files, chat messages or tickets.
- Laptops are full-disk encrypted; loss is reported to Rohit Kulkarni within **two hours**.
- Client code is not published to public repositories under any circumstances.
- Suspected phishing is forwarded to security@dydtech.in and then deleted.

Breach of the information security rules is treated as gross misconduct.

## 7. Code of conduct

Harassment, discrimination and retaliation are grounds for immediate dismissal. Complaints go
to Nisha Gupta (HR Manager) or, where HR is implicated, directly to Meera Joshi (CEO).
Complaints are investigated within ten business days and the complainant is told the outcome.
