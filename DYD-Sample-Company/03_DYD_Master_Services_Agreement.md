# Master Services Agreement — DYD Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

**Document:** DYD-MSA-2026 · **Version:** 3.1 · **Effective:** 1 January 2026
**Legal owner:** Sneha Raut (Company Secretary, DYD Technologies)

This agreement governs all engagements between DYD Technologies Pvt. Ltd. and its clients.
Individual Statements of Work incorporate these terms by reference.

---

## 1. Service levels and availability

DYD commits to the availability targets below, measured monthly, excluding scheduled
maintenance notified at least five business days in advance.

| Service tier | Monthly availability target | Maximum scheduled maintenance |
|---|---|---|
| Platinum | 99.9% | 4 hours per month |
| Gold | 99.5% | 8 hours per month |
| Silver | 99.0% | 12 hours per month |

## 2. Service credits

Where measured availability falls below the contracted target in any calendar month, the
client is entitled to a service credit against the following month's fee.

| Tier | Availability achieved | Service credit |
|---|---|---|
| Platinum | Below 99.9% but at or above 99.5% | 5% of monthly fee |
| Platinum | Below 99.5% but at or above 99.0% | 10% of monthly fee |
| Platinum | Below 99.0% | 20% of monthly fee |
| Gold | Below 99.5% but at or above 99.0% | 5% of monthly fee |
| Gold | Below 99.0% | 10% of monthly fee |
| Silver | Below 99.0% | 5% of monthly fee |

Service credits are capped at 20% of the monthly fee in any single month and at 50% of annual
fees across a contract year. Credits must be claimed in writing within 30 days of the month
in which the shortfall occurred; unclaimed credits lapse.

Service credits are the client's sole and exclusive financial remedy for availability
shortfalls. They do not apply to downtime caused by client infrastructure, third-party carrier
outages, or force majeure.

## 3. Response time commitments

| Severity | Response | Resolution target |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | 30 minutes | 4 hours |
| P2 | 4 business hours | 2 business days |
| P3 | 1 business day | 10 business days |
| P4 | 3 business days | Next scheduled release |

## 4. Intellectual property

Custom code written specifically for a client under a Statement of Work transfers to that
client on full payment. Pre-existing DYD frameworks, libraries and tooling remain DYD property
and are licensed to the client perpetually, non-exclusively and royalty-free for use with the
delivered system.

DYD retains the right to reuse general skills, know-how and techniques acquired during an
engagement.

## 5. Confidentiality

Each party protects the other's confidential information with no less care than it applies to
its own, and in no case less than reasonable care. Obligations survive termination by three
years. Client data is never used to train machine-learning models without separate written
consent.

## 6. Data protection

DYD processes client personal data only on documented instruction. Sub-processors require
prior written approval. Personal data of Indian data principals is stored within India
(AWS ap-south-1) unless the client instructs otherwise in writing.

Breach notification to the client is within **24 hours** of DYD becoming aware.

## 7. Termination

Either party may terminate for convenience with 90 days' written notice, or immediately for
material breach not cured within 30 days of written notice. On termination the client pays for
work performed to the termination date. DYD provides a 30-day transition assistance period at
standard rates.

## 8. Limitation of liability

Each party's aggregate liability is capped at the fees paid under the relevant Statement of
Work in the twelve months preceding the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect or
consequential loss, including loss of profit or anticipated savings. This cap does not apply
to breach of confidentiality, infringement of intellectual property, or wilful misconduct.
